--- Remote Work Policy ---
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
Requests must be submitted through the FlexWork portal by Thursday of the
preceding week. Remote employees must be reachable on Microsoft Teams during
agreed working hours and use the TigerLink VPN for internal systems. New hires
must work fully on-site during their first 30 calendar days.

--- Hybrid Work Guidelines ---
Siam Innovate uses a hybrid working model. Core collaboration hours are 10:00
to 16:00 Bangkok time, during which employees must be available for meetings
regardless of location. Tuesday is the company-wide office anchor day, and
teams may define one additional anchor day. Office desks must be reserved in
the SpaceLy app at least one day in advance.

--- Annual Leave ---
Full-time employees receive 15 days of paid annual leave per calendar year,
accrued at 1.25 days per month. The entitlement increases to 20 days after
five full years of service. Up to 5 unused days may be carried into the first
quarter of the following year and expire on 31 March. Leave requests must be
submitted through LeaveDesk at least 3 business days in advance for absences
of 3 days or fewer and 14 days in advance for longer absences.

--- International Travel Approval Process ---
Employees traveling internationally for business must obtain written approval
from their department head at least 14 days before departure. Requests are
submitted through TravelHub under "Overseas Trip Request" with a trip purpose,
itinerary, and cost estimate. Trips longer than 10 business days also require
Managing Director approval. Once approved, the Travel Desk books economy-class
airfare through the contracted travel agency.

--- International Travel Daily Allowance ---
Approved international business travel receives a daily allowance of 2,400 THB
for meals and incidental expenses. Departure and return days receive half the
daily rate, or 1,200 THB. Hotel caps are 5,500 THB per night in Southeast Asia
and 8,000 THB elsewhere. Hotel and ground-transport receipts must be uploaded
to ExpenseFlow within 45 days after returning.

--- International Travel Insurance ---
International business trips are automatically covered by the SafeJourney
Plan B group travel insurance. Coverage includes emergency medical treatment
up to 3,000,000 THB, emergency evacuation, and lost-luggage compensation up to
40,000 THB per trip. Claims must be filed through the SafeJourney portal within
30 days of returning, with original receipts and the TravelHub approval.

--- Expense Reimbursement ---
Business expenses must be claimed through ExpenseFlow with scanned receipts
within 45 days of the expense date. Claims below 20,000 THB require line-manager
approval; claims of 20,000 THB or more also require Finance approval. Approved
amounts are paid with the next payroll. Alcohol, traffic fines, minibar charges,
and personal subscriptions are not reimbursable.

--- PaySiam Gateway Product Overview ---
PaySiam Gateway is an online payment platform for Thai small and medium
merchants. It supports PromptPay QR, domestic and international cards, mobile
banking transfers, and installment plans. Standard pricing is 1.85% per
successful domestic card or QR transaction and 2.95% for international cards,
with no setup fee or monthly minimum. Settlement uses a T+2 business-day cycle.

--- Customer Support Service Levels ---
Standard product support operates from 09:00 to 18:00 Bangkok time on working
days. Platinum support operates around the clock. For a P1 production outage,
the first-response target is 15 minutes for Platinum and 1 hour for Standard.
For a P2 major degradation, the targets are 1 hour and 4 hours respectively.
Cases are created in the customer portal and receive a CS-XXXXXX tracking
number.

--- Support Escalation Process ---
Customers or account managers may escalate a support case through the customer
portal or escalations@siaminnovate.example using the CS case number. Escalated
cases are owned by the duty manager. A P1 case open for more than 4 hours
automatically starts a bridge call and status updates every 30 minutes until
service is restored. A cause summary is completed within 5 business days after
an escalated case closes.
