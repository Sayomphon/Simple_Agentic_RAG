--- PaySiam Gateway Product Overview ---
PaySiam Gateway is Siam Innovate's online payment acceptance platform for
Thai small and medium merchants. It supports PromptPay QR, domestic and
international cards, mobile banking transfers, and installment plans from
partner banks. Pricing is 1.85% per successful domestic card or QR
transaction and 2.95% for international cards, with no setup fee and no
monthly minimum on the standard plan. Settlement to the merchant's bank
account follows a T+2 business day cycle. Merchant onboarding requires
business registration documents and is completed within 3 business days of
document approval. Developers integrate through the DevSiam portal, which
provides a sandbox environment, API keys, and plugins for popular Thai
storefront platforms. Refunds are free within 30 days of the original
transaction.

--- StockSense Product Overview ---
StockSense is a subscription analytics service that helps retail chains
forecast demand and rebalance inventory between branches. It ingests sales
data hourly — including point-of-sale feeds from PaySiam Gateway — and
produces reorder recommendations, dead-stock alerts, and seasonal demand
forecasts tuned to Thai holiday patterns. Three plans are offered: Starter
at 4,900 THB per month for up to 2 branches and 5,000 SKUs; Growth at
12,900 THB per month for up to 10 branches and 50,000 SKUs; and Enterprise
with custom pricing, unlimited branches, and a dedicated success manager.
A 21-day free trial includes onboarding assistance. Forecast accuracy
statistics are shared with customers quarterly, and historical data is
retained for 3 years on all plans.

--- ChatServe Product Overview ---
ChatServe is Siam Innovate's Thai-language customer conversation platform.
Businesses deploy it on LINE Official Accounts, Facebook Messenger, and a
website widget to answer routine questions, track orders, and hand over to
human agents with full conversation context. The standard plan costs
15,000 THB per month and includes 20,000 automated conversations;
additional conversations are billed at 0.90 THB each. ChatServe ships with
pre-trained Thai intents for retail, food delivery, and financial services,
and merchants using PaySiam Gateway can let customers check payment status
inside the chat. Typical onboarding, including intent tuning with the
customer's historical chat logs, takes 2 weeks. Conversation transcripts
are stored in Thailand and retained for 13 months by default.

--- Customer Support Service Levels ---
Siam Innovate offers two support plans for its products. Standard support
operates during business hours, 09:00 to 18:00 Bangkok time on working
days, through the customer portal and support@siaminnovate.example.
Platinum support operates around the clock and adds the dedicated hotline
02-099-4477. Cases are prioritized on four levels: P1, a production service
fully unavailable — first response within 15 minutes on Platinum and 1 hour
on Standard; P2, a major function degraded — 1 hour on Platinum and 4 hours
on Standard; P3, a minor function affected — 4 hours and 1 business day
respectively; and P4, questions and change requests — 2 business days on
both plans. Targets are measured from case creation in the portal, and
every case receives a tracking number in the format CS-XXXXXX. These
targets form the service level commitments referenced in customer
contracts.

--- Support Escalation Process ---
If a support case misses its response target, or its business impact grows,
customers or account managers request escalation through the customer
portal or by emailing escalations@siaminnovate.example with the CS case
number. Escalated cases are owned by the duty manager, who reassigns
specialists and posts progress updates on the case. A P1 case open for more
than 4 hours automatically triggers a bridge call led by the duty manager,
with customer participation offered, and public status updates every
30 minutes on status.siaminnovate.example until service is restored.
Platinum customers are additionally assigned an executive sponsor who joins
quarterly service reviews. After any escalated case closes, the support
team completes a cause summary within 5 business days and shares it with
the customer on request.

--- Service Credit Policy ---
Service availability commitments are 99.9% per calendar month for Platinum
customers and 99.5% for Standard, measured per product and excluding the
announced maintenance window on Sundays from 01:00 to 05:00 Bangkok time.
When measured availability falls below the commitment, customers may claim
service credits: 5% of that month's subscription fee when availability
stays above 99.0%, 10% for availability between 97.0% and 99.0%, and 25%
below 97.0%. Credit claims are submitted through the customer portal within
30 days of the end of the affected month, referencing related CS case
numbers where available. Credits are applied to a future invoice, never
paid in cash, and are capped at 25% of one month's fee. Availability
statistics per product are published monthly on the status page.
