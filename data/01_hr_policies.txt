--- Company Overview ---
Siam Innovate Co., Ltd. is a Thai enterprise software company founded in 2013
and headquartered at One Innovate Tower on Rama IX Road, Bangkok. A second
product development studio operates in Chiang Mai, opened in 2021. The company
employs around 480 people across engineering, product, customer operations,
and corporate functions. Siam Innovate builds and operates three commercial
products: PaySiam Gateway for online payment acceptance, StockSense for retail
inventory analytics, and ChatServe for Thai-language customer conversation
automation. The fiscal year follows the calendar year, and company-wide
results are shared at quarterly town halls held in the Rama IX auditorium and
streamed to the Chiang Mai studio.

--- Remote Work Policy ---
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
Remote work requests are submitted through the FlexWork portal by Thursday
of the preceding week. Approval is at the manager's discretion and may be
withdrawn if collaboration or performance suffers. Remote employees must be
reachable on Microsoft Teams during their agreed working hours and must use
the TigerLink VPN when accessing internal systems. New hires in their first
30 calendar days are expected to work fully on-site to support onboarding.
Working remotely from outside Thailand for more than 5 consecutive business
days requires prior written approval from both HR and the Legal team.

--- Hybrid Work Guidelines ---
Siam Innovate operates a hybrid model: staff can work off-site up to 3 days
each week, subject to manager approval requested via the FlexWork portal no
later than Thursday of the week before. In addition, all employees observe
core collaboration hours from 10:00 to 16:00 (Bangkok time), during which
they must be available for meetings regardless of location. Tuesdays are
designated company-wide anchor days, when all teams work from the Rama IX
headquarters. Teams may define one additional anchor day of their own. Desk
booking for office days is done through the SpaceLy app at least one day in
advance.

--- Annual Leave ---
Full-time employees receive 15 days of paid annual leave per calendar year,
accrued at 1.25 days per month. After five full years of service, the
entitlement increases to 20 days. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over
into the first quarter of the following year; carried-over days expire on
31 March. Leave requests are submitted in the LeaveDesk system at least
3 business days in advance for absences of 3 days or fewer, and at least
14 days in advance for longer absences. Managers must approve or decline
requests within 2 business days. Unused leave beyond the carry-over limit
is forfeited without compensation.

--- Sick Leave ---
Employees are entitled to paid sick leave of up to 30 working days per year,
in line with Thai labor law. Absences of 3 or more consecutive working days
require a medical certificate from a licensed physician, uploaded to
LeaveDesk within 5 days of returning to work. For same-day sick notice,
employees must inform their manager via Teams or phone before 10:00. Sick
leave cannot be converted to cash or carried over. Employees who exhaust
their sick leave entitlement may apply for unpaid medical leave, which
requires HR approval and, for absences beyond 30 additional days, an
assessment by the company's occupational health partner, MediTrust Clinic.

--- Parental Leave ---
Pregnant employees are entitled to 98 days of maternity leave per pregnancy,
of which 45 days are paid at full salary by the company and the remainder
covered under Social Security rules. Fathers employed by Siam Innovate
receive 15 working days of paid paternity leave, to be used within 90 days
of the child's birth. Employees adopting a child under age 7 receive 30
calendar days of paid adoption leave. All parental leave is coordinated
through HR at least 30 days before the expected start date where possible,
and recorded in LeaveDesk under the "Family" category. Returning parents may
request a temporary 4-day work week for up to 6 months, subject to manager
approval.

--- Bereavement and Compassionate Absence ---
Employees are entitled to compassionate absence of up to 7 working days, at
full pay, following the passing of an immediate family member — a spouse,
child, or parent. For grandparents, siblings, or parents-in-law, the
entitlement is 3 working days. The absence is recorded in LeaveDesk under
the "Compassionate" category, and supporting documents may be requested by
HR afterwards rather than in advance. Employees traveling to ceremonies
outside Bangkok may combine the compassionate absence with other accrued
time off with manager approval, and additional unpaid days can be granted
by the HR Business Partner. Colleagues wishing to arrange a company wreath
contact the People Care team through SiamPeople.

--- Ordination Leave ---
Employees who wish to enter the Buddhist monkhood may take up to 15 working
days of paid ordination absence once during their employment with Siam
Innovate. Requests are recorded in LeaveDesk under the "Ordination" category
at least 30 days in advance, together with the planned ceremony date and
temple. The benefit is available after probation confirmation. A
confirmation letter from the temple is submitted through LeaveDesk within
7 days of returning to work. Longer ordination periods can be arranged as
unpaid personal absence with HR approval, up to a maximum of 120 days. The
absence does not reduce other time-off entitlements, and public holidays
falling within the period are not counted against the 15 days.

--- Overtime Policy ---
Non-exempt employees who work beyond standard hours with prior manager
approval are compensated per Thai labor law: 1.5x the hourly rate on
workdays, 2x for work on weekly holidays, and 3x for overtime on public
holidays. Overtime must be requested and approved in the TimeTrack system
before the work is performed; retroactive claims are accepted only for
documented production emergencies. Exempt employees (manager level and
above) are not eligible for overtime pay but may claim compensatory time
off for weekend releases, capped at 6 days per year. Sustained overtime
above 24 hours per month triggers a workload review between the manager
and HR Business Partner.

--- Company Holidays ---
Siam Innovate observes 16 paid public holidays per year, published each
November for the following year on the SiamPeople app. The list always
includes New Year's Day, Songkran (13-15 April), Labour Day, and His
Majesty the King's Birthday. When a public holiday falls on a weekend, a
substitute day off is granted on the next working day. The office closes
early at 15:00 on the last working day before New Year and before Songkran.
Employees required to work on a public holiday due to production duty
receive holiday overtime rates per the Overtime Policy plus a substitute
day off to be used within 60 days.

--- Compensation Disbursement Schedule ---
Monthly remuneration is disbursed on the 28th calendar day of each month.
When the 28th falls on a weekend or public holiday, disbursement moves to
the preceding business day. Funds are transferred to the Thai bank account
registered in SiamPeople, and itemized statements appear in SiamPeople
under "My Compensation" one day before disbursement. Overtime recorded and
approved in TimeTrack by the 15th of the month is included in that month's
disbursement; later approvals roll into the following month. Questions
about deductions, provident fund contributions, or tax withholding are
directed to the People Operations team through SiamPeople. Off-cycle
corrections are processed only for discrepancies exceeding 1,000 THB;
smaller differences are adjusted in the following month.

--- Provident Fund ---
Siam Innovate operates a registered provident fund managed by KrungSiam
Asset Management and administered through the FundLink portal. Enrollment
opens upon probation confirmation, effective from the first day of the
following month. Employees contribute between 3% and 8% of monthly base
compensation; the company matches 3% during the first two years of service,
5% from the third year, and 7% from the sixth year. Company contributions
vest at 50% after 2 full years of fund membership and 100% after 4 years.
Contribution rates can be changed twice a year, in June and December,
through FundLink. Three investment plans are offered: Steady (fixed
income), Balanced, and Horizon (equity-weighted). Departing employees
choose between a payout and a transfer to a new employer's fund within
60 days.

--- Health Benefits ---
After probation confirmation, employees are enrolled in the VitalCare Gold
group health plan administered by Bangkok Assurance PCL. The plan covers
outpatient care up to 2,500 THB per visit (maximum 30 visits per year),
inpatient hospitalization up to 500,000 THB per condition, and annual dental
care up to 8,000 THB. An annual health checkup at BCH Wellness Center is
provided every January at no cost. Employees may add a spouse and up to
3 children to the plan at a subsidized premium of 4,200 THB per dependent
per year, deducted from payroll. Claims are submitted through the VitalCare
mobile app; cashless service is available at network hospitals listed in
the SiamPeople benefits page.

--- Wellness and Fitness Benefit ---
Under the FitSiam program, employees may claim up to 800 THB per month for
sports and fitness activities, including gym memberships, fitness classes,
swimming pools, and organized runs. Claims are submitted through ExpenseFlow
with receipts within 45 days, and unused monthly amounts do not accumulate.
Eligibility begins after probation confirmation. The program also funds one
company sports day, MoveFest, held each November at a rented stadium, and
quarterly step challenges run through the SiamPeople app with prizes for
the top three teams. Equipment purchases such as shoes or rackets are not
eligible under FitSiam, and family members are not covered.

--- Employee Assistance Program ---
Through the MindBridge partnership, all employees and their immediate
family members can access confidential guidance sessions with licensed
practitioners for personal or work-related difficulties. Each employee
family is entitled to 6 sessions per calendar year at no cost, in Thai or
English, in person or by video call. Appointments are made directly with
MindBridge by calling internal extension 5-4477 or through the MindBridge
web portal linked from SiamPeople; managers and HR are never informed who
has used the service. MindBridge practitioners can refer employees to
MediTrust Clinic for further assessment where appropriate. In urgent
situations outside office hours, the MindBridge line operates around the
clock, every day of the year.

--- Onboarding Process ---
New employees join through the LaunchPad onboarding program. Before day one,
IT ships a configured laptop and HR sends a welcome pack through the
SiamPeople app. The first week includes orientation sessions on company
values, security (the SecureBase course must be completed within 7 days),
and tooling. Every new hire is paired with a LaunchPad buddy — a colleague
outside their direct team — for the first 60 days. Managers must schedule a
30-60-90 day check-in series in GrowthCompass. New hires work fully on-site
for their first 30 calendar days, after which standard hybrid rules apply.
Building access cards are issued by Facilities on day one at the Rama IX
reception.

--- Probation Period ---
All new employees serve a probation period of 119 days from their start
date. During probation, either party may terminate employment with 7 days'
written notice. A mid-probation review is held around day 60, and the final
confirmation review must be completed in GrowthCompass no later than day
105, so HR can process the outcome before the legal deadline. Probationary
employees accrue annual leave normally but may only take it after day 60,
except with special manager approval. Successful confirmation unlocks
benefits enrollment in the VitalCare Gold health plan and the provident
fund, both effective from the first day of the month following confirmation.

--- Performance Review Cycle ---
Performance is reviewed twice a year in GrowthCompass: a mid-year review in
June and a full-year review in December. Each cycle includes a self-review,
peer feedback from at least 2 colleagues, and a manager assessment against
the goals agreed at the start of the year. Ratings use a 4-point scale:
Exceptional, Strong, Developing, and Off-Track. Employees rated Off-Track
enter a 60-day performance improvement plan with biweekly check-ins. Salary
adjustments and bonus decisions follow the December cycle and take effect
with the February payroll. Promotion cases are prepared by managers and
reviewed by the cross-functional Talent Council, which meets each January
and July.

--- Training Budget ---
Each employee has an individual learning budget of 25,000 THB per calendar
year, managed through the SkillForge platform. The budget covers courses,
certifications, conferences, and professional books. Single items above
15,000 THB require manager approval in SkillForge before purchase.
Certification exam fees are covered in full and do not draw down the
personal budget if the certification appears on the company's Priority
Skills List, published each January by the L&D team. Unused budget does not
roll over. Employees who resign within 6 months of completing a
company-funded certification costing more than 20,000 THB must repay 50% of
the cost, as set out in the training agreement signed at enrollment.

--- Employee Referral Program ---
Employees who refer a successful candidate receive a referral bonus:
30,000 THB for engineering and product roles and 15,000 THB for all other
positions. The referral is registered before the candidate's first
interview by submitting Form HR-204 in SiamPeople, naming the candidate and
the vacancy. The bonus is paid with the monthly disbursement after the new
hire completes the 119-day probation period. Referrals of former employees
are eligible only if the person left more than 12 months earlier. Members
of the Talent Acquisition team, hiring managers filling their own
vacancies, and executives are not eligible. There is no limit on the number
of referrals per employee; in 2025 the top referrer placed four successful
hires.

--- Internal Transfer Program ---
Employees may apply for internal vacancies after 12 months in their current
role, or earlier with their current manager's written consent. Openings
open to internal applicants are listed in the SiamPeople careers tab, and
applications are submitted with Form HR-119. The Talent Acquisition team
informs the current manager only after a first interview has taken place.
Transfers take effect on the first day of a month, with a handover period
of up to 4 weeks agreed between the two managers. A transfer does not
restart probation and does not reduce accrued time-off entitlements;
compensation is reviewed only when the new role carries a different job
level. Employees on an active performance improvement plan may not
transfer until the plan is closed.

--- Workplace Attire Standards ---
Standard working attire at Siam Innovate offices is business casual:
collared shirts, blouses, polos, chinos, skirts, or tailored trousers.
Denim without rips is acceptable on Fridays together with the company polo.
Formal business attire — a suit or jacket — is expected when meeting
clients, whether at a client site or when hosting visitors at the office.
T-shirts with slogans, shorts, sandals, and sportswear are not appropriate
on office days, except during MoveFest and team sports activities.
Closed-toe footwear is required in the server room and the facilities
workshop. Teams with frequent client-facing duties may define stricter
norms with HR agreement. Religious and cultural garments are always
welcome.

--- Grievance and Misconduct Reporting ---
Employees who experience or observe workplace misconduct, unfair treatment,
or behavior inconsistent with the company's code of conduct can raise the
matter through EthicsLine. Reports are made by email to
ethicsline@siaminnovate.example, by calling internal extension 5-8800, or
anonymously through the EthicsLine web form. Every report is acknowledged
within 2 business days and reviewed by the HR Business Partner together
with, where needed, the Ethics Committee, and an initial outcome is
communicated within 15 business days. Retaliation against anyone who raises
a concern in good faith is itself a disciplinary offense. Concerns about
one's own line manager may be raised directly with the next-level manager
or through EthicsLine without informing the line manager first.

--- Resignation Process ---
Employees who wish to resign must give at least 30 days' written notice,
submitted through SiamPeople with a signed resignation letter. The final
working date is confirmed by HR after checking outstanding leave and
project handover plans. Departing employees complete a handover document
approved by their manager, return all equipment and access cards to IT and
Facilities, and attend an exit interview with the HR Business Partner in
the final week. Unused annual leave within the statutory entitlement is
paid out with the final salary. Access to all systems, including SiamPeople
and ExpenseFlow, is revoked at 18:00 on the last working day; pending
expense claims must be submitted before then.

--- IT Security and Password Policy ---
All company accounts must use passwords of at least 16 characters generated
and stored in the company password manager, ShieldPass. Multi-factor
authentication via the OktaSiam app is mandatory for email, ExpenseFlow,
and all production systems. Sharing credentials is a disciplinary offense.
Phishing simulations are run monthly by the Security team; employees who
fail three simulations in a rolling 12-month period must retake the
SecureBase awareness course. Suspected security incidents must be reported
to soc@siaminnovate.example within 1 hour of discovery. USB storage devices
are blocked by default on all company laptops and require a time-limited
exemption approved by the CISO.

--- Security Incident Response Process ---
Suspected security incidents must be reported to soc@siaminnovate.example
within 1 hour of discovery, or by calling the Security Operations Center at
internal extension 5-2222, which is staffed around the clock. The SOC
classifies each incident by severity: SEV-1 covers confirmed exposure of
Restricted data or a production outage caused by an attack, with a response
within 30 minutes; SEV-2 covers a contained compromise of a single account
or device, with a 2-hour response; SEV-3 covers policy violations without
data exposure, handled the next business day. An incident commander is
assigned to every SEV-1 and SEV-2 case to coordinate containment,
communication, and recovery. A post-incident review is completed within
5 business days, and employees must not discuss incidents with anyone
outside the response team.

--- Data Classification and Handling ---
Policy POL-DC-03 defines four data classification tiers. Public information
may be shared freely. Internal information — most working documents — is
available to all employees but not to outsiders. Confidential information,
such as unreleased product plans, customer contracts, and financial
results, is limited to named individuals or teams and must be stored in
approved systems with encryption at rest. Restricted information, including
customer payment data and personnel records, may exist only in designated
production systems and never in email, chat, or personal storage. Document
owners label files with the tier in the header or filename and review
classifications every March. Moving data to a lower tier requires the data
owner's written approval. Questions go to the Security team via HelpHub.

--- Acceptable Use of AI Tools ---
Employees may use only the AI services listed in the AI Register,
maintained by the Security team in HelpHub. The register includes the
company-hosted assistant SiamAssist and a small set of approved cloud
services listed per team. Public and Internal data may be entered into
registered tools; Confidential and Restricted data must never be entered
into any AI service, in line with POL-DC-03. Requests to add a tool to the
AI Register are submitted through HelpHub and reviewed by the Security team
within 10 business days. AI-generated code must pass the same review
process as human-written code before merging, and AI-generated text used in
client documents must be checked by the document owner. Violations are
treated as security policy breaches.

--- VPN Usage ---
Access to internal systems from outside the office network requires the
TigerLink VPN client, installed by IT on all company devices. Employees sign
in with their ShieldPass-managed credentials plus OktaSiam multi-factor
authentication. The VPN session limit is 12 hours, after which
re-authentication is required. Split tunneling is disabled; all traffic
routes through the corporate gateway while connected. Personal devices may
not connect to TigerLink except under the BYOD program, which requires
device enrollment in the MobiGuard management platform and annual security
attestation. VPN connection issues are reported to the IT Service Desk via
the HelpHub portal, with a target response time of 4 business hours.

--- BYOD Program ---
Under the BYOD program, employees may use a personal smartphone or tablet
for company email, Teams, and the SiamPeople app after enrolling the device
in the MobiGuard management platform and completing the annual security
attestation. Enrollment separates a managed company profile from personal
data; Security can wipe only the company profile. Minimum platform versions
are published in HelpHub and enforced automatically at sign-in. Personal
laptops may connect to TigerLink only when enrolled under the same program
and individually approved by the CISO. A lost or stolen enrolled device
must be reported to the IT Service Desk within 24 hours so the company
profile can be wiped. Leaving the program, or leaving the company, triggers
removal of the company profile within one business day.

--- Software Request and Licensing ---
Standard software titles are listed in the HelpHub Software Catalog and
installed within 2 business days of a request, with no further approval
needed. Titles outside the catalog require a license review by IT and
approval from the requesting department's cost center owner. IT completes
license reviews within 5 business days and negotiates volume terms when
more than 10 seats are requested. All installed software is inventoried by
the MobiGuard management platform, which automatically removes unlicensed
installations after a 7-day warning to the device owner. License renewals
are consolidated each June, when department heads confirm continued need.
Open-source components used inside Siam Innovate products follow a separate
review handled by the engineering platform team.

--- Equipment and Laptop Policy ---
New employees choose between a MacBook Air and a Lenovo ThinkPad X1 as their
standard machine; engineering roles may request a higher-spec build via the
HelpHub portal. Laptops are refreshed every 3 years, and the old device must
be returned to IT for certified data wiping. Employees receive a one-time
home-office allowance of 12,000 THB, claimable through ExpenseFlow within
the first 90 days of employment, covering a monitor, chair, or desk
accessories. All devices are enrolled in the MobiGuard management platform
and must not be altered or jailbroken. Damaged or lost equipment must be
reported to the IT Service Desk within 24 hours; negligent loss may result
in a cost-sharing charge of up to 20% of replacement value.

--- Office Access and Visitor Policy ---
Building access badges are issued by Facilities at the Rama IX reception on
an employee's first day and activated for the floors their role requires.
A lost or damaged badge is reported to Facilities at internal extension
5-1100; the first replacement is free and each subsequent replacement costs
300 THB. Visitors are pre-registered in the SpaceLy visitor module at least
one day ahead, sign in at reception, wear a visitor badge, and are escorted
by their host at all times. Holding the door open for an unknown person —
tailgating — is prohibited; everyone badges in individually. Access rights
are reviewed quarterly by Facilities together with team managers, and
badges of departing employees are collected on the final working day.

--- Meeting Room and Desk Booking ---
Workspace reservations are made in the SpaceLy app. Desks for office days
are booked at least one day in advance, in line with the Hybrid Work
Guidelines. Meeting rooms can be reserved up to 30 days ahead; rooms with
video equipment are named after Thai rivers — Chao Phraya, Ping, Wang, Yom,
and Nan — and Nan is prioritized for client meetings. A room booking is
automatically released if nobody checks in on the room panel within
10 minutes of the start time. Full-day bookings and recurring bookings
beyond 4 weeks require Facilities approval. External guests attending a
meeting must be pre-registered through the SpaceLy visitor module. Catering
for meetings of 10 or more people is ordered through Facilities at least
2 business days ahead.

--- Parking and Commuting ---
The One Innovate Tower garage offers employee car parking at 1,500 THB per
month, deducted from monthly compensation, with allocation managed through
a Facilities waitlist that currently runs 2 to 3 months. Motorcycle parking
on level B2 is free and needs no registration. Eight electric-vehicle
charging bays on level 2 are reserved through SpaceLy for up to 4 hours per
session at no charge. Visitor parking validation stamps are available at
reception for client visits. A free shuttle van runs between the MRT Phra
Ram 9 station and the tower every 20 minutes from 07:00 to 10:00 and from
16:00 to 19:30 on working days. Parking at the Chiang Mai studio is free
and unallocated.

--- Emergency and Evacuation Procedures ---
The internal emergency line, extension 5-9999, is answered around the clock
by building security and connects to medical, fire, and safety responders.
When the fire alarm sounds, employees leave via the stairwells — never the
lifts — and gather at Assembly Zone C in the open-air parking area on the
Rama IX service road, where floor wardens in orange vests take a headcount.
Evacuation drills are held twice a year, in May and October, and
participation is mandatory. First-aid rooms are located on floors 5 and 12,
and automated external defibrillators hang beside the lift lobby on every
third floor. Employees with temporary mobility limitations register with
Facilities so wardens can assist them during an evacuation. Emergencies
during business travel are coordinated through the SafeJourney assistance
line shown on the digital insurance card.
