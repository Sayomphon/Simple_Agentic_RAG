--- International Travel Approval Process ---
Employees traveling internationally for business must obtain written approval
from their department head at least 14 days before departure. All requests are
submitted through the TravelHub system under "Overseas Trip Request" and must
include a trip purpose statement, an itinerary, and a cost estimate. Trips
longer than 10 business days additionally require sign-off from the Managing
Director. Once approved, the Travel Desk books economy-class airfare through
the company's contracted agency, ThaiOrbit Travel. Self-booked flights are not
reimbursable unless the Travel Desk confirms in writing that no contracted
option was available. Visa fees and airport transfers are arranged by the
Travel Desk and charged directly to the department's cost center.

--- International Travel Daily Allowance ---
For approved international business trips, Siam Innovate provides a daily
allowance of 2,400 THB to cover meals and incidental expenses. The allowance
is paid per full calendar day abroad; departure and return days are paid at
half rate (1,200 THB). Hotel accommodation is booked by the Travel Desk with
a cap of 5,500 THB per night in Southeast Asia and 8,000 THB per night
elsewhere. No receipts are required for the daily allowance itself, but hotel
and ground transport receipts must be uploaded to ExpenseFlow within 45 days
of the return date. Allowances are deposited together with the following
month's payroll.

--- International Travel Insurance ---
All international business trips are automatically covered by the company's
group travel insurance, the SafeJourney Plan B underwritten by Bangkok
Assurance PCL. Coverage includes emergency medical treatment up to
3,000,000 THB, emergency evacuation, and lost-luggage compensation up to
40,000 THB per trip. Employees must carry the digital insurance card, which
is available in the HR mobile app (SiamPeople) under "My Benefits". Claims
must be filed through the SafeJourney portal within 30 days of returning,
together with original receipts and a copy of the trip approval from
TravelHub. Personal side-trips extending a business trip are not covered
beyond 48 hours after the last business day.

--- International Travel Visa Support ---
For destinations requiring an entry visa, the Travel Desk prepares and
submits the application on the employee's behalf. Employees complete Form
TR-77 (Visa Assistance Request) in TravelHub at least 21 days before the
planned departure and provide a passport valid for at least 6 more months.
Visa fees are charged directly to the department's cost center, consistent
with other travel arrangements. Expedited processing within 10 days is
possible only with Managing Director approval. Invitation and employment
confirmation letters are issued by the Legal team within 3 business days of
a request. If a visa is refused, the Travel Desk cancels the bookings at no
cost to the employee and informs the department head so the trip can be
rescheduled or reassigned.

--- Domestic Travel Policy ---
Business travel within Thailand must be approved by the employee's line
manager at least 5 business days before departure, submitted through the
TravelHub system under "Domestic Trip Request" with a trip purpose statement
and a cost estimate. Overnight domestic trips qualify for a daily allowance
of 800 THB per full day and 400 THB for departure and return days. Hotels are
booked through the Travel Desk with a cap of 2,200 THB per night nationwide.
Rail and bus tickets are booked in standard class; domestic flights
additionally require department head approval. Mileage for personal-car
trips follows the Expense Reimbursement rate of 6 THB per kilometer. Hotel
and transport receipts must be uploaded to ExpenseFlow within 45 days of
return, except for the receipt-free daily allowance.

--- Client Site Visit Policy ---
Same-day visits to client premises within the Bangkok metropolitan area do
not require a TravelHub request. Employees notify their line manager and the
account manager at least 2 business days in advance and record the visit in
the shared client calendar. Taxi and ride-hailing fares to and from the
client site are reimbursed through ExpenseFlow with receipts; there is no
daily allowance for same-day visits. Visits lasting more than one day, or
outside the Bangkok metropolitan area, follow the Domestic Travel Policy
instead. Employees wear formal business attire at client premises, carry
their Siam Innovate badge, and follow the client's on-site security rules.
Client-issued visitor badges must be returned before leaving the site.

--- Expense Reimbursement ---
Business expenses are reimbursed through the ExpenseFlow system. Employees
must submit claims with scanned receipts within 45 days of the expense date;
late claims are rejected automatically unless the CFO grants an exception.
Claims under 20,000 THB are approved by the line manager; claims of
20,000 THB or more also require Finance approval. Approved amounts are paid
with the next payroll run. Non-reimbursable items include alcohol (except
approved client entertainment), traffic fines, minibar charges, and personal
subscriptions. Client entertainment above 5,000 THB per event requires
pre-approval from the department head. Mileage for personal-car business
trips is reimbursed at 6 THB per kilometer.

--- Corporate Credit Card ---
Employees at manager level and above may apply for a KrungSiam Platinum
corporate credit card through the Finance Service Desk. The standard monthly
limit is 150,000 THB; temporary increases for approved events or business
trips can be requested via ExpenseFlow at least 5 business days in advance.
Cardholders must reconcile all transactions in ExpenseFlow within 45 days,
attaching itemized receipts — the same deadline that applies to out-of-pocket
claims. Personal use of the corporate card is strictly prohibited and leads
to card cancellation on the second violation. Lost or stolen cards must be
reported to KrungSiam Bank's 24-hour hotline and the Finance Service Desk
within 24 hours.

--- Petty Cash Policy ---
Each Siam Innovate office keeps a petty cash float — 8,000 THB at the
Rama IX headquarters and 4,000 THB at the Chiang Mai studio — held by a
designated custodian in the Finance team or the local office manager.
Petty cash covers small, immediate purchases up to 1,000 THB per
transaction, such as courier fees, minor office supplies, or taxi fares for
urgent errands. Each disbursement requires Form FIN-07, a numbered voucher
signed by the recipient with the original receipt attached. The float is
reconciled and replenished monthly, and Finance audits the vouchers
quarterly. Petty cash may not be used for travel allowances, client
entertainment, or anything reimbursable through ExpenseFlow, and shortfalls
are investigated by the Financial Controller.

--- Purchase Requisition and Purchase Orders ---
Purchases from vendors are initiated with Form FIN-31, the purchase
requisition, in the ProcureDesk system. Requisitions up to 50,000 THB need
approval from the requester's department head. Requisitions above
50,000 THB additionally require Finance approval and result in a formal
purchase order, which must be issued before the vendor starts work or ships
goods. Requisitions above 100,000 THB require three competing quotations
attached in ProcureDesk, and above 500,000 THB the CFO signs the purchase
order personally. Splitting a purchase into smaller requisitions to avoid
an approval step is prohibited. In documented emergencies, a purchase may
proceed with verbal Finance approval and must be ratified in ProcureDesk
within 5 business days. Goods receipt is confirmed in ProcureDesk before
invoices are paid.

--- Vendor Onboarding and Registration ---
Before a first purchase order can be issued, every new vendor must be
registered in ProcureDesk. The vendor provides a company affidavit issued
within the last 6 months, VAT registration (Por Por 20), a bank account
confirmation letter, and a signed copy of the Siam Innovate supplier code
of conduct. The Procurement team screens new vendors within 10 business
days, including sanctions and litigation checks for contracts expected to
exceed 1,000,000 THB per year. Vendors with no transactions for 24 months
are set to inactive and must be re-verified before new orders. Changes to
a vendor's bank details take effect only after a call-back to a previously
registered contact person — never on the basis of an emailed request alone.

--- Vendor Invoice and Payment Terms ---
Siam Innovate's standard payment term is Net 45 from receipt of a correct
tax invoice. Vendors send invoices to ap@siaminnovate.example quoting the
purchase order number; invoices without a valid purchase order reference
are returned unprocessed. Payment runs are executed on the 15th and the
last business day of each month, by bank transfer only. The Accounts
Payable helpdesk can be reached at internal extension 5-3300 for status
questions. Early payment within 15 days can be agreed against a 2% discount
for orders above 200,000 THB, at Finance's discretion. Withholding tax
certificates are issued automatically with each payment. Disputed invoices
are flagged in ProcureDesk and paused until the requester confirms the
dispute is resolved.
